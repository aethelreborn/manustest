This policy explains what Aethel collects, why it is needed, and the controls you have.

1. What we collect

When you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication provides your Google account identifier, email address, display name, and profile image when available. We use this information to create and secure your Aethel account.

2. Your vault data

Vault secrets are encrypted on your device before optional sync. The server stores encrypted ciphertext and its initialization vector, not your plaintext passwords, cards, or notes. Your local vault key is kept in device-secure storage on supported native platforms.

3. Bills and focus data

Billing reminders, focus sessions, preferences, and settings may be stored locally. If you are signed in, the app may sync the non-secret record needed to provide cross-device continuity. You can continue using the core app offline.

4. Service providers

Firebase Authentication processes Google sign-in. Aethel’s SQL service stores account records and encrypted sync data. These providers process data only to operate the features described here and under their own applicable policies.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you remove it or uninstall the app. To request account and server-side data deletion, open an issue in the Aethel repository issue tracker. Never include passwords, recovery phrases, or vault secrets in a support request.

6. Security limits

No service can promise absolute security. Keep your device updated, protect your Google account, and never share your recovery material. Aethel will not ask for your vault password or plaintext secrets.

7. Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests can be submitted through the Aethel repository issue tracker. This policy may be updated when the product or its data practices change.
